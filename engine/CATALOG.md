# The 108 — effect catalog (12 categories × 9)

Each line gives the effect's id, then its working name, then the mechanism to build. Builders may refine the mechanism if
that makes the effect better, but they must keep the id and the core idea.
✅ = already built (reference effect).

## hook — cold opens. These shots must read in the first 2 s; frame 0 must work as a thumbnail.
- `hook.price-strike` — Price Strike: a big agency-style price and timeline ("$ / weeks") is set in display type. A hand-drawn strike line slashes it, and the replacement value (copy.after) springs up beneath.
- `hook.number-slam` — Number Slam: a single giant number counts up fast, then lands with a scale-overshoot impact (camera shake ≤ 6 px for 150 ms). The label tucks in beneath.
- `hook.question-type` — Question Type-on: copy.question types character by character with a blinking caret. The last word re-colours to the accent with a soft underline sweep.
- `hook.split-contrast` — Split Contrast: the frame splits into two panels, before vs after, with labels and copy.before / copy.after. The divider slides to give the "after" side the win.
- `hook.countdown` — Countdown: 3-2-1 digits morph one into the next (outline-to-fill), each with a ring tick. Zero collapses into the logo.
- `hook.notification-flood` — Notification Flood: generic notification cards (icon, title, time) stack up faster and faster until the screen is full. Everything freezes, then clears to a one-line hook.
- `hook.pixel-zoom-out` — Zoom From a Pixel: frame 0 is one glowing pixel. The camera pulls out through scales of a grid until the full headline is revealed.
- `hook.search-query` — Search Query: a generic search bar. The query types itself, suggestion rows drop down, and one suggestion highlights (the brand).
- `hook.myth-fact-flip` — Myth / Fact: a card reading a common belief flips in 3D (perspective squash) to reveal the fact side, which is accent-tagged.

## logo — reveals
- `logo.particle-assemble` ✅
- `logo.stroke-draw` — Stroke Draw: the mark's outline draws itself with a travelling bright head. The fill wipes in behind it and the wordmark fades up letter-staggered.
- `logo.light-sweep` — Light Sweep: the mark is revealed out of near-darkness by a diagonal specular band sweeping across. Rim light lands last.
- `logo.grid-snap` — Grid Snap: a grid of tiles (sampled from the mark mask) flies in from scattered positions and snaps into place with a stepped stagger.
- `logo.liquid-fill` — Liquid Fill: an outlined mark fills from the bottom with an animated wave surface (two sine layers), then settles flat.
- `logo.shard-assemble` — Shard Assemble: triangular shards rotate in from depth and lock together to form the mark. There is a soft flash on lock.
- `logo.ink-bloom` — Ink Bloom: organic noise-edged blobs bloom outward, inked in the brand colours, masking the mark into view. Works light and dark.
- `logo.glass-bevel` — Glass Bevel: the mark is rendered as faux-3D glass (layered offsets, inner highlight, refraction tint) on a slow tilt with a moving highlight.
- `logo.orbit-lock` — Orbit Lock: 3 thin rings orbit in perspective, collapse inward and lock. The mark appears at the moment of lock.

## type — kinetic typography
- `type.baseline-rise` ✅
- `type.beat-words` — Beat Words: a sentence is shown ONE word at a time at a big size, cut on a 120 BPM grid. The final word gets the accent.
- `type.weight-morph` — Weight Morph: a word's weight animates from hairline to heavy (variable font) while tracking tightens. One emphasis word swells.
- `type.split-flap` — Split Flap: a departures-board style. Each character flips through letters top-half/bottom-half until it lands on the headline.
- `type.tracking-breathe` — Tracking Breathe: widely-tracked caps contract to tight display tracking as the opacity resolves. The line then gently breathes.
- `type.scramble-decode` — Scramble Decode: glyph slots cycle random characters (seeded) left-to-right, resolving into the headline. Mono for the scramble, display at rest.
- `type.oversize-pan` — Oversize Pan: one huge word (much bigger than the frame) pans slowly across. The crop reveals the brand colour inside the counters. Intentionally offstage.
- `type.echo-stack` — Echo Stack: the headline repeats in stacked outline copies that collapse into one solid line (a depth echo).
- `type.highlighter` — Highlighter: a sentence sits still while a marker band sweeps under the key phrase, and that phrase's colour inverts on the band.

## ui — product UI showcase (generic, invented-for-demo UI only; no real products)
- `ui.window-tilt` — Window Tilt: a browser window drawn in code rotates in 3D from steep perspective to flat while its content cascades in.
- `ui.cursor-walkthrough` — Cursor Walkthrough: a pointer glides along a bezier path, hovers a button (hover state), clicks (ripple), and the UI reacts with a new state.
- `ui.zoom-to-feature` — Zoom to Feature: a full dashboard. The camera pushes into one card while the rest blurs and dims; a label pops at the end.
- `ui.state-morph` — State Morph: one container morphs (size, radius, content) through button → card → modal → toast. It is continuous and never cuts.
- `ui.scroll-reel` — Scroll Reel: a tall landing page scrolls smoothly inside a device frame with inertia, and sections parallax.
- `ui.phone-orbit` — Phone Orbit: a phone frame swings in with a 3D yaw (perspective-skewed slab). The screen shows an app home that comes alive.
- `ui.terminal-run` — Terminal Run: a terminal window types a command, output lines stream, a progress bar fills, and a green check finishes it.
- `ui.bento-build` — Bento Build: a bento grid of 5–6 feature tiles assembles with staggered spring scale. Each tile has an icon and a short label.
- `ui.chat-thread` — Chat Thread: a chat interface. The user's message appears, a typing indicator pulses, and the assistant reply streams word by word.

## feature — callouts
- `feature.callout-lines` — Callout Lines: hairline leader lines draw from points on a UI mock out to labels, one at a time, with dots pulsing at the anchors.
- `feature.spotlight` — Spotlight: the frame dims except a soft-edged spotlight that travels between 3 regions; captions follow it.
- `feature.magnifier` — Magnifier: a circular lens glides over dense UI and shows 2.2× magnified detail inside, with a lens rim highlight.
- `feature.before-after-wipe` — Before/After Wipe: a draggable divider handle wipes from the "before" (grey, cluttered) to the "after" (clean, accent) version.
- `feature.steps-123` — Steps 1-2-3: three numbered steps light up in turn along a connecting line that fills between them. The labels come from copy.features.
- `feature.icon-grid` — Icon Grid: 4–6 line icons draw on with stroke animation in a staggered grid, labels beneath.
- `feature.toggle-flip` — Toggle Flip: a big toggle switch flips with a spring. The whole scene recolours from off-state grey to the brand accent.
- `feature.checklist` — Checklist: items appear and get checked off one by one (check draws on, row slides slightly). The last item gets the accent.
- `feature.card-carousel` — Card Carousel: feature cards ride a 3D carousel (cylinder projection); the front card highlights and its label reads.

## data — proof
- `data.counter-roll` ✅
- `data.bar-rise` — Bar Rise: bars grow from the baseline with staggered outQuint and the values count up. The brand bar wins in accent; the axis is honest (zero baseline).
- `data.line-draw` — Line Draw: a growth line draws left to right with a glowing lead dot. Area fill fades in beneath, and a milestone label pins at the end.
- `data.donut-fill` — Donut Fill: a ring fills to a percentage with a round cap while the centre number counts, plus a small legend.
- `data.kpi-tiles` — KPI Tiles: 3–4 metric tiles pop in with delta chips (▲ +x%) and tiny sparklines drawing.
- `data.unit-dots` — Unit Dots: a 10×10 dot matrix fills to show a proportion; the filled dots ripple in order.
- `data.dot-globe` — Dot Globe: a rotating dot-sphere globe (Fibonacci points, orthographic). Location pins rise with pulses and an arc connects two pins.
- `data.rank-race` — Rank Race: horizontal bars reorder as values change over time, and the brand climbs to #1. All data is sample data from copy.
- `data.milestone-stairs` — Milestone Stairs: a stepped path climbs with milestone flags popping at each step (dates/labels), and the camera follows upward.

## social — proof from people (fictional sample people only)
- `social.quote-card` — Quote Card: a large quote mark draws on, the quote text reveals by line mask, and a name/role with an initials avatar slides in.
- `social.post-cascade` — Post Cascade: generic social-post cards (avatar initials, handle, text, like count) cascade down in two columns with depth.
- `social.review-marquee` — Review Marquee: two rows of review cards scroll in opposite directions. The frame edges fade, and one card enlarges.
- `social.star-rating` — Star Rating: five stars fill left to right with a sweep to 4.9, the number counts, and a "from N reviews" line follows.
- `social.logo-wall` — Logo Wall: a grid of abstract placeholder company marks (geometric, invented) fades in. One cell glows: "teams like yours".
- `social.wall-zoom` — Wall Zoom: a dense wall of tiny testimonial cards; the camera dives into one, which becomes readable.
- `social.avatar-crowd` — Avatar Crowd: initial-avatars pop in a growing cluster (Vogel spiral) until the count lands: "N teams".
- `social.case-flip` — Case Flip: a customer card (fictional company) flips to reveal the result metric in big type.
- `social.press-ticker` — Press Ticker: a slow horizontal ticker of short pull-quotes attributed to invented publications with generic names.

## transition — shot-to-shot (draw(g, t, {a, b}))
- `transition.object-bridge` — Object Bridge: a rounded rectangle grows from the centre of A to fill the frame, revealing B inside it (a continuity bridge).
- `transition.shape-morph` — Shape Morph: a circle mask from A morphs to a rounded rect, then expands to full frame, containing B.
- `transition.zoom-through` — Zoom Through: A scales up rapidly toward the camera, with radial motion streaks, and fades as B scales in from slightly smaller.
- `transition.mask-wipe` — Mask Wipe: a diagonal soft-edged wipe with a thin accent leading edge.
- `transition.light-flash` — Light Flash: A blooms to a bright exposure peak; B emerges from the exposure.
- `transition.pixel-dissolve` — Pixel Dissolve: an ordered dither dissolve in growing blocks, from A to B.
- `transition.shared-axis-push` — Shared-Axis Push: A pushes out left with a slight scale-down while B pushes in from the right; motion-blur friendly.
- `transition.liquid-wipe` — Liquid Wipe: a wavy edge (noise displaced) sweeps upward, revealing B.
- `transition.iris` — Iris: an iris closes to a point on A's focal point, then opens on B.

## background — atmospheres (usually `ownsBackground: true`, loopable)
- `background.gradient-mesh` ✅
- `background.dot-parallax` — Dot Parallax: three layers of dot grids drift at different speeds with depth fade.
- `background.aurora` — Aurora: soft vertical light curtains (shader) wave slowly in brand hues.
- `background.depth-field` — Depth Field: a starfield-like point field streams toward the camera with depth-of-field size and alpha.
- `background.fog-noise` — Fog Noise: layered fbm fog drifts, lit from one side by the accent.
- `background.flow-lines` — Flow Lines: thousands of thin curl-noise streamlines drift like combed silk.
- `background.contours` — Contours: animated topographic contour lines (shader iso-lines of slowly evolving noise).
- `background.horizon-grid` — Horizon Grid: a perspective floor grid recedes to a soft horizon glow and scrolls forward. Restrained, not retro-neon.
- `background.glass-blobs` — Glass Blobs: frosted translucent blobs float over a gradient with refraction-like highlights.

## hero3d — dimensional hero objects (faux-3D with projection math or shaders)
- `hero3d.glass-stack` — Glass Stack: 4 translucent glass cards are stacked in perspective, fan out, and settle, with a highlight sweeping across.
- `hero3d.extruded-type` — Extruded Type: a headline extruded in depth (layered offsets with shading) rotates gently and catches light.
- `hero3d.orbit-cards` — Orbit Cards: small feature cards orbit a central mark on a tilted ellipse with depth sorting and scale.
- `hero3d.particle-sphere` — Particle Sphere: a rotating sphere of points breathes, then morphs into the logo's points.
- `hero3d.pedestal-sweep` — Pedestal Sweep: a hero object (the logo as a thick plate) sits on a lit disc while a rim light sweeps around.
- `hero3d.exploded-layers` — Exploded Layers: a UI card separates into its layers (bg, image, text, buttons) along z in isometric view, then recomposes.
- `hero3d.holo-panel` — Holo Panel: a floating translucent panel with scanline shimmer and an edge glow shows a headline. Tasteful, not sci-fi kitsch.
- `hero3d.ribbon-flow` — Ribbon Flow: a wide ribbon (a strip mesh drawn as quads) twists through 3D space with brand gradient shading.
- `hero3d.cube-wave` — Cube Wave: an isometric grid of cubes rises and falls in a travelling wave and resolves into a flat plane.

## cta — offer and close
- `cta.price-reveal` — Price Reveal: the plan name, then the price slams in with a digit roll, then the period and the "per month" settle.
- `cta.plan-cards` — Plan Cards: three plan cards rise in; the middle one lifts, scales and gets the accent border plus a "Most popular" chip.
- `cta.free-stamp` — Free Stamp: a rubber-stamp "FREE" (or copy.cta) hits with an impact squash, ink texture and a slight rotation.
- `cta.url-type` — URL Type-on: the domain types in a pill-shaped address bar, a cursor clicks "Go", and the pill glows.
- `cta.button-press` — Button Press: a big CTA button is pressed by the pointer (depress plus a ripple), and confetti-free success feedback (a check) follows.
- `cta.launch-countdown` — Launch Countdown: a DD:HH:MM:SS countdown with flipping digits plus copy.launchDate.
- `cta.waitlist-counter` — Waitlist Counter: an avatar stack grows while the counter ticks "N people on the waitlist", then "Join them".
- `cta.end-lockup` — End Lockup: the closing card. Mark plus wordmark centred, tagline beneath, URL pill; elements settle with micro-stagger and hold.
- `cta.date-lockup` — Date Lockup: a big launch date, with the month and day animating in on a calendar-tile flip; the tagline sits beneath.

## loop — social formats (seamless loops where `loop: true`)
- `loop.logo-loop` — Logo Loop: the mark rotates through 3 colourways with a seamless 6 s loop (a GIF-friendly, mostly static camera).
- `loop.caption-burst` — Caption Burst: vertical-video style. Captions pop word-group by word-group with a bold keyword highlight box.
- `loop.story-bars` — Story Bars: segmented progress bars at the top fill while 4 slides (feature cards) advance.
- `loop.expectation-reality` — Expectation vs Reality: a two-panel compare with labels; the "reality" panel is the brand's clean result.
- `loop.swipe-carousel` — Swipe Carousel: square slides swipe horizontally with pagination dots, like a carousel post turned video.
- `loop.sticker-pop` — Sticker Pop: die-cut stickers (icons plus words) pop on with slight rotations and a white border, then peel off. Loopable.
- `loop.ticker-tape` — Ticker Tape: two angled marquee bands of brand words cross the frame forever (a seamless loop).
- `loop.quad-loop` — Quad Loop: a 2×2 grid of mini-scenes (icon animations) loops in sync.
- `loop.badge-pulse` — Badge Pulse: a "NEW" badge pulses with ring waves beside a feature name (a seamless loop).
