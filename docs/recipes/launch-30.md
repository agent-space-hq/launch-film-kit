# Recipe: Launch film, 30 s (default)

The default for a product launch.

| Beat | Time | Job | Good library shots |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0–3 s | Stop the scroll. Frame 0 = thumbnail | `hook.number-slam`, `hook.question-type`, `hook.price-strike`, `hook.split-contrast` |
| Turn | 3–6 s | Name the problem or the change | `type.baseline-rise`, `type.highlighter`, `hook.myth-fact-flip` |
| Reveal | 6–9 s | Brand / product appears | `logo.*`, `ui.window-tilt`, `hero3d.glass-stack` |
| Beat 1 — what | 9–13 s | What it does | `ui.cursor-walkthrough`, `ui.state-morph`, `ui.chat-thread` |
| Beat 2 — how | 13–17 s | How it works / why it's easy | `feature.steps-123`, `feature.callout-lines`, `ui.bento-build` |
| Beat 3 — why | 17–21 s | Why it's better | `feature.before-after-wipe`, `feature.toggle-flip`, `type.beat-words` |
| Proof | 21–25 s | A real number or quote | `data.counter-roll`, `data.bar-rise`, `social.quote-card`, `social.star-rating` |
| CTA | 25–30 s | Brand + URL, hold ≥ 2.5 s | `cta.end-lockup`, `cta.url-type`, `cta.date-lockup` |

Transitions: choose 2 at most, for example `transition.object-bridge` and `transition.shared-axis-push`. Score: sparse until the reveal, build through the beats, resolve on the end card.
